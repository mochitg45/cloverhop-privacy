Privacy Policy — Clover Hop: Lucky Bunny Idle

Effective 16 October 2026. Applies to the Android app with the identifier com.inatasunsoft.cloverhop, published by Inata Sun Soft. The same policy will apply to an iPhone and iPad version if one is released.

Clover Hop is a single-player idle game. There is no account, no sign-up and no login. We do not ask for your name, your email address or anything else about you, and the game does not send your progress anywhere. This policy explains what stays on your device and the small amount of data that the advertising and purchase services inside the app handle.

What the app itself collects

Nothing. Your progress (stage, coins, upgrades, album, wardrobe and settings) is saved in your device's private app storage and stays there. We run no server and keep no database of players. Uninstalling the app deletes your progress.

Advertising choices

You can reset or delete your Advertising ID at any time in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Google's consent form is shown before the first ad request, and you can reopen it from "Privacy options" in the game's Settings.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the services above, contact us and we will help you raise it with Google or RevenueCat.

Your rights and how to request data deletion

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to export or erase. Everything the app stores is on your device and is removed by uninstalling the app. For data held by AdMob, reset your Advertising ID or use Google's privacy controls at myaccount.google.com/data-and-privacy. To have your RevenueCat purchase record deleted, email us with the app and the approximate purchase date and we will request it.
